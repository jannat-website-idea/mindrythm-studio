2. Understanding How Your CMS Works

Real-Time Autosave & The Draft Sync Engine

Unlike standard older websites (WordPress) where mistakes can easily break the live page, your site is powered by Next.js + Sanity Content Lake. Here is the golden mechanism configured specifically for you:

🚀 You Don't Have to Fear Losing Work

  • Every keystroke is autosaved: As you type a new title, upload a photo, or change a price, Sanity automatically saves your changes to cloud servers every millisecond.
  • Instant Live Draft Reflection: We configured a secure server viewer token (perspective: "drafts") on your live server. Even if you forget to click the green "Publish" button, your changes are already queued and reflected on the live site!
  • Best Practice: Always click the green "Publish" button in the bottom right corner when you are completely finished with an item. This seals the version and clears the "Draft" tag.

3. Step-by-Step Instructions for Everyday Tasks

1
How to Log In to the CMS Studio

1. Open your browser and go to https://mindrythm-cms.sanity.studio.

2. Click "Log in with Google" or "Log in with Email" using admin@mindrythm.com.

3. You will immediately land on your clean visual dashboard showing all content categories in the left sidebar.

2
How to Add or Edit a Service

1. In the left sidebar, click on "Services".

2. To edit an existing service (e.g., Commercial Video or 3D CGI Animation), click on its name.

3. To add a new one, click the "+" (Create New) button at the top.

4. Fill in: Title → Click "Generate" next to Slug → Write the Short Description → Add Features.

5. Click the green "Publish" button in the bottom right corner.

3
How to Add a Photo or Video to the Bento Gallery

1. In the left sidebar, click "Gallery".

2. Click the "+" (Create) button.

3. Enter a Title (e.g. Studio BTS - Fashion Shoot).

4. Select Type: Choose "Image" or "Video".

5. Choose Layout Size:
• Normal (1x1): Standard square/vertical card.
• Featured Large (2x2): Takes double width/height in the grid for hero visuals.

6. Drag and drop your image (JPG/PNG/WebP) or MP4 video file.

7. Click "Publish".

4
How to Add or Update a Portfolio Project

1. In the left sidebar, click "Projects".

2. Click "+" to create a project or click an existing one.

3. Enter Title, click Generate for Slug, enter Client Name and Category.

4. Upload the Hero Cover Image and any supporting project gallery shots.

5. Click "Publish".

5
How to Update Social Media Links & Contact Phone

1. In the left sidebar, click "Site Settings".

2. Update your phone number, studio address, or social links (Instagram, LinkedIn, Behance, YouTube).

3. Click "Publish". Your footer and contact page update immediately.

4. Media Upload Rules & Guidelines (Read Before Uploading)

90% of website issues happen when files are in the wrong format or too large. Follow these golden rules to keep the website lightning fast and flawless across all iPhones, Androids, and Macs.

✅ DO USE THESE FORMATS

  • Photos: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
  • Videos: .mp4 or .webm (Encoded with H.264 codec)
  • Dimensions:
    • Bento Grid Large: 1200 × 1200 px
    • Bento Grid Standard: 800 × 800 px
    • Hero Project Covers: 1920 × 1080 px
  • Max File Size: Under 3 MB for images; Under 20 MB for background videos.

❌ NEVER USE THESE FORMATS

  • NO iPhone .heic photos: Apple iPhones save photos as HEIC by default. Web browsers CANNOT display HEIC. Always export as JPG!
  • NO RAW camera files: Never upload .CR2, .NEF, .ARW, or uncompressed .TIFF files.
  • NO 4K/8K 500MB Video Files: Huge video files take 45 seconds to load and consume client mobile data. Always compress to 1080p MP4 before uploading.
  • NO Special Characters in file names: Avoid names like Shoot#1 (final)@2.jpg. Use mindrythm-shoot-01.jpg.
⚠️ How to Convert iPhone HEIC to JPG in 3 Seconds:
If taking photos from an iPhone: In iPhone Settings → Camera → Formats → choose "Most Compatible".
Or use free Mac Preview / Web tool: export as JPG before uploading to Sanity.

6. Deployment & Hostinger VPS Management

Do I Need to Deploy Every Time I Edit Text or Photos?

NO! You do NOT need to deploy for CMS edits.

Your website is connected in real-time to Sanity CMS. Whenever you edit text, upload an image, or change a service in Sanity Studio, your live website updates automatically. There is no deployment or coding needed for day-to-day operations.

When Is a Server Deployment Needed?

A deployment is ONLY needed if you modify the underlying software code (e.g. altering the fundamental layout structure, installing new software plugins, or changing API configurations).

How You Can Run a 1-Click Refresh on Hostinger Yourself

If you ever want to restart or refresh the website server on Hostinger without developer assistance, follow these exact 4 steps:

1
Log in to Hostinger hPanel

Go to https://hpanel.hostinger.com → Log in → Navigate to VPS → Select your server (86.38.243.142).

2
Open the Browser Terminal / Console

Click on "Terminal" or "SSH Access" right inside your Hostinger dashboard (no special software needed).

3
Run the 1-Line Update Command

Copy and paste this single command into the terminal and press Enter:

cd ~/mindrythm-studio && git pull && npm run build && pm2 restart mindrythm

4
Done! Website Refreshed

The terminal will show [PM2] Process mindrythm restarted. Your website is instantly running the fresh build with zero downtime.

7. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Q: I published a new photo, but when I open mindrythm.com I still see the old one?

Answer: Your browser has cached the old page. Simply hard refresh by pressing Ctrl + Shift + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + R (Mac), or open the site in an Incognito/Private window. It will immediately show the new photo.

Q: My video uploaded, but it shows a black screen or won't play on iPhone?

Answer: The video was saved in an unsupported format (like Apple ProRes or AVI). Export the video in Premiere / Final Cut / CapCut as H.264 MP4 with standard AAC audio. Keep the resolution at 1080p and file size under 20MB.

Q: I created a new Service or Project, but it isn't showing up on the homepage?

Answer: Check two things in Sanity Studio:
1. Did you click the "Generate" button next to the Slug field? Every item requires a slug (e.g. /commercial-video).
2. Did you click the green "Publish" button at the bottom right corner?

Q: How do I invite another team member to edit content in Sanity?

Answer: Go to https://sanity.io/manage → Select Project mindrythm-studio → Click "Members" → Click "Invite New Member" → Enter their email address → Assign role as Editor or Administrator.

Q: How do we renew the domain or SSL certificate?

Answer: Both your domain (mindrythm.com) and SSL security certificate are hosted and auto-renewed in your Hostinger account. As long as your payment method is active in Hostinger, SSL renewals happen automatically.
