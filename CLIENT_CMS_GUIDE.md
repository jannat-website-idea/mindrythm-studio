# Mindrythm CMS — Client Guide

## Parindey Productions · Full access guide (edit, upload, delete)

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open **https://mindrythm-cms.sanity.studio** in Chrome or Safari.
2. Sign in with your studio email and password.
3. You will land on a clean menu that lists every content area of the website.

---

## 3. Editing content (the basics)

1. Click a menu item, for example **Services**.
2. Click a specific entry to open the editor.
3. Change any field: text boxes, dropdowns, image uploads, ratings.
4. Click **Publish** in the top-right corner. Until you publish, your
   changes remain a draft.
5. Done. The website updates automatically — no developer required.

### Handy tips

- Fields with a **red asterisk** are required; the document will not
  publish until they are filled.
- Character limits (for example max 60) show a counter as you type.
- **Undo / redo** buttons sit at the top of the editor.
- The **History** panel (clock icon) restores any earlier version of a
  document.
- Press **Publish** whenever you are happy; drafts are invisible to the
  public website.

---

## 4. Adding new content (new project, gallery item, service…)

1. Open the section, for example **Portfolio / Projects**.
2. Click **Create new** in the top-right corner.
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — what visitors see.
   - **Project ID / Item ID** — auto-generates from the title and is
     used in the URL. Keep it short, lowercase, no spaces.
   - **Display order** — lower numbers appear first (10, 20, 30…).
   - **Media** — see the upload section below.
   - **Category / year / accent** — styling details; pick anything.
4. Click **Publish**.

---

## 5. Uploading images and videos

Any **Media** field (projects, gallery, team, hero) works the same way:

1. Click the upload area, or drag and drop a file.
2. For images, use the **hotspot / crop** tool to choose which part of
   the photo is shown.
3. Fill in the **Accessible description (alt text)** — required.
   Example: "Aerial view of a tropical shoreline at golden hour".
4. In the **Gallery**, choose **Image or Video** (MP4 / WebM / MOV) and
   **Layout type: Large or Small**. This selects the bento-grid slot
   size. The grid itself stays fixed — you only choose the slot.
5. You can also paste an **existing media URL** instead of uploading.

---

## 6. Deleting content (fully enabled for you)

1. Open the section, for example **Gallery**.
2. Open the item you want to remove.
3. Click the **⋯** (three-dot) menu in the top-right corner.
4. Choose **Delete**, then confirm.
5. The item disappears from the website immediately.

Notes:

- Delete works for **Services, Projects, Gallery items, Testimonials,
  and Team members**.
- Single-page settings (Hero, Footer, Contact, SEO, Privacy, Terms)
  cannot be deleted — they are meant to be edited, not removed.
- If you delete a project that was featured in the Hero, the homepage
  automatically fills from your remaining projects.

---

## 7. Preview before publishing (Presentation)

Click the **Presentation** icon in the top bar. You will see your draft
rendered on the actual website before publishing — perfect for checking
how a new gallery item or hero image looks first.

---

## 8. Extra useful features

- **Draft vs published** — save drafts anytime; the public site only
  shows published content.
- **Duplicate** — the ⋯ menu can duplicate any document. Great for
  creating similar projects or gallery items quickly.
- **Media library** — the Assets tab lists every uploaded image and
  video in one place. Re-use, replace, or remove files.
- **Live updating** — the website reflects published changes instantly.
- **Reordering** — use the "Display order" numbers (1, 2, 3…); no
  dragging needed.
- **Comments** — leave notes on any field for your team.

---

## 9. If something looks wrong on the website

Check in this order:

1. Did you click **Publish**? Drafts never show on the site.
2. Are all required fields (red asterisk) filled?
3. Is the image/video actually uploaded (or a URL present) in the Media
   field?
4. Is the "Display order" a reasonable number? (999 puts the item last.)

---

## 10. Support

If anything is still unclear, contact your development team with:
- The section you were using (e.g. "Gallery").
- What you tried to do (e.g. "Delete a video").
- The exact message shown on screen.
